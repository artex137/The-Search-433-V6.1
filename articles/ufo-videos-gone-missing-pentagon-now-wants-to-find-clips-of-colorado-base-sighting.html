UFO Videos Gone Missing? Pentagon Now Wants To Find Clips Of Colorado Base Sighting

The Pentagon is investigating the disappearance of two cellphone videos that reportedly show a 100-foot triangular UFO flying over a military base in Colorado. The videos were recorded by a service member during two separate nights in 2023. No injuries or fatalities have been reported in connection with the sightings.

This investigation signifies an increased interest in unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP) by the U.S. government. Pentagon Press Secretary Joel Valdez stated that the department is "actively investigating" the status of the videos, which have not yet been made public. Valdez added that findings will be released under the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE).

Key facts

  • Incident occurred in Colorado in 2023.
  • The videos were recorded on consecutive nights.
  • The object is described as a roughly 100-foot triangular shape.
  • Footage is reportedly held by the Air Force Office of Special Investigations.
  • The videos are referenced under file numbers IRR1669S015524 and IRR1669S005024.
  • The Pentagon has not confirmed the existence or content of the videos.

Timeline

In 2023, a service member recorded two videos allegedly showing a triangular UFO near a military base in Colorado. The footage has since been classified and not disclosed to the public. On August 16, 2026, the Pentagon announced its investigation into the whereabouts of these videos.

Official statements

“We will release what we find under PURSUE on war.gov/ufo,” said Pentagon Press Secretary Joel Valdez.

Context

This investigation follows previous releases of UAP-related files by the Trump administration, aimed at increasing transparency regarding unidentified aerial phenomena. The latest records included a reported triangular UFO sighting near Colorado Springs, which involved a former Pentagon employee's account of observing a translucent object.

What’s next

The Pentagon's investigation is ongoing. It remains to be seen whether the videos will be released to the public or if further information will be disclosed regarding their content and classification status.
