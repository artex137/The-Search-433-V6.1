GoblinCon Invades Hopkinsville Once Again

Hopkinsville is hosting the second annual GoblinCon UFO and Paranormal Expo, which runs through Sunday at The Bruce. The event celebrates the 71st anniversary of the Kelly-Hopkinsville encounter, a significant UFO incident from 1955. There have been no reported injuries or fatalities associated with the convention.

The GoblinCon aims to highlight the community's historical connection to the infamous encounter and has attracted a diverse group of paranormal enthusiasts, UFO researchers, and curious visitors. Organizers have expanded the event this year to include new attractions and programming, reflecting its growing popularity.

Key facts

  • Date and Time: August 22-23, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday and 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. Sunday.
  • Location: The Bruce Convention Center, Hopkinsville, Kentucky.
  • Vendors: Approximately 65 to 70 vendors and interactive exhibits.
  • Admission: $10 for a single day; $20 for both days; VIP package for $150.
  • Livestream: Available for $10 on the GoblinCon website.
  • Organizers: Visit Hopkinsville.
  • Notable Participants: Bigfoot researchers David Eller and Ken Gerhard, UFO researchers Sam Maranto and Tim Swartz, and Geraldine Sutton Stith.

Timeline

The GoblinCon is a continuation of the community's efforts to celebrate the Kelly-Hopkinsville encounter, which occurred on August 21, 1955. The initial GoblinCon took place in October 2025, but organizers moved the event to August to coincide with the anniversary. This year's convention has seen significant growth, with the number of vendors and interactive attractions increasing from the inaugural event.

On Saturday, attendees can explore a variety of presentations and panels covering topics such as UFOs, ghosts, and Bigfoot. New attractions this year include a museum-style walkthrough featuring artifacts related to the 1955 encounter and a miniature replica of the farmhouse where the incident occurred.

Official statements

“The response to this year’s convention has exceeded expectations,”

said Amy Rogers, executive director of Visit Hopkinsville.

“A major shout-out to the Bruce Convention Center, because let me tell you, these guys and girls here are crushing it.”

Context

The Kelly-Hopkinsville encounter is one of Kentucky's best-known UFO legends, involving reports of strange creatures around a farmhouse. The incident has become a staple of local folklore and continues to attract interest from researchers and enthusiasts alike. GoblinCon aims to solidify this connection through annual celebrations.

What’s next

Organizers hope to make GoblinCon a regular part of Hopkinsville's observance of the Kelly-Hopkinsville anniversary in the coming years. Future plans for the convention may include expanded programming and increased community involvement.
