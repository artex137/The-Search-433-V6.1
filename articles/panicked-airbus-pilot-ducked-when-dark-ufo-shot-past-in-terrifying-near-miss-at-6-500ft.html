Panicked Airbus pilot ducked when dark UFO shot past in terrifying near miss at 6,500ft

Published 2026-08-23 00:00 UTC

Airbus Pilot Ducks as UFO Passes at 6,500 Feet Over London

An Airbus pilot ducked in panic when a dark UFO flew “within metres” of the aircraft over London on June 1. The incident occurred as the A320 was descending to Heathrow Airport at an altitude of 6,500 feet, but no injuries were reported.

This incident raises concerns about aviation safety and the potential risks posed by unidentified flying objects. The UK Airprox Board has launched an official investigation into the near miss, noting a “high” risk of collision.

Key facts

  • Incident date: June 1, 2023
  • Location: Stamford Hill, London
  • Aircraft type: Airbus A320
  • Altitude: 6,500 feet
  • Risk of collision: High, according to the UK Airprox Board
  • Previous incidents: Two other near misses reported earlier this year

Timeline

The Airbus A320 was making its descent to Heathrow Airport when the incident occurred. According to the report, the Captain was the pilot flying (PF) and ducked in response to the sudden appearance of a “black object” that passed to the left of the flight deck. The First Officer, who was not looking up at the time, only caught a glimpse of the object as it flew by.

Safety investigations revealed that radar analysis did not show any primary or secondary contacts associated with the object at the time of the event. The Board noted that the details of the object were insufficient to determine its nature.

Official statements

“The A320 pilot reports descending through roughly 6500ft downwind to RW27R, when a black object passed to the left of the flight deck by no more than a few metres,” the report stated.
“In the Board’s opinion the reported altitude and/or description of the object were such that they were unable to determine the nature of the unknown object,” the report added.

Context

This incident is part of a growing concern regarding unidentified flying objects in UK airspace. Earlier this year, a Royal Air Force (RAF) pilot also experienced a near miss when a UFO flew close to a Voyager tanker during a refuelling operation at 16,000 feet off the Norfolk coast. That object, described as “black and circular,” also did not appear on radar.

What’s next

The UK Airprox Board continues to investigate the incident. Further analysis may provide insights into the nature of the unidentified object and the implications for aviation safety.
