Department of War investigating mysterious triangle UFO videos after Post report

Published 2026-08-15 23:52 UTC

Department of War investigating triangle UFO videos

The Department of War has launched an investigation into claims that the Air Force is withholding two cellphone videos showing a 100-foot triangle UFO over a Colorado military base. This announcement follows a report by The Post on August 15, 2026, regarding the alleged cover-up. Acting Pentagon Press Secretary Joel Valdez confirmed the agency's active inquiry on Wednesday.

This investigation is significant as it pertains to ongoing efforts to disclose information about unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP). Valdez stated that the findings will be released under the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE) on the government’s designated website.

Key facts

  • Investigation initiated by the Department of War.
  • Two cellphone videos reportedly recorded in 2023.
  • Videos show a triangle UFO near a military base in Colorado.
  • Videos stored at the Air Force Office of Special Investigations.
  • File numbers for the videos: IRR1669S015524 and IRR1669S005024.
  • No timetable for the release of the videos has been disclosed.

Timeline

In July 2023, government employees reportedly witnessed a triangle UFO near Colorado Springs. On August 15, 2026, The Post published a report alleging that the Air Force is covering up two videos of the incident. Following the report, the Department of War announced it would investigate the claims.

On August 16, 2026, Valdez confirmed that the department is actively looking into the whereabouts of the videos and plans to release findings through PURSUE.

Official statements

“We will release what we find under PURSUE on war.gov/ufo,” said Valdez.

Context

The investigation is part of a broader effort by the Trump administration to increase transparency regarding UFO encounters. Recently, the administration announced that it would waive non-disclosure agreements for former government employees and contractors to encourage the sharing of information about UFO programs.

The previous release of UFO files by the government has attracted significant public interest, with billions of hits on related websites.

What’s next

The Department of War will continue its investigation into the videos, with no set timeline for when they may be made public. Further updates are expected as the investigation progresses.
