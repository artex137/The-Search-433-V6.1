New government UFO files include Colorado Springs sightings

DENVER — The Pentagon has released a new batch of declassified UFO files, which includes two reported sightings from Colorado Springs. The documents were made public on Friday, August 7, 2026, and comprise 41 items related to unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAPs).

This release is significant as it contributes to the ongoing investigation into UAPs and reflects the government's transparency efforts regarding unexplained aerial phenomena. The files encompass reports from various locations, including Montana, Utah, Brazil, Sweden, Afghanistan, the Pacific Ocean, and the Middle East.

Key facts

  • Release date: August 7, 2026
  • Number of documents: 41
  • Reported sightings in Colorado Springs: 2
  • First sighting: "Dark Translucent Triangle" reported in October 2023
  • Second sighting: "Large Triangle with Red Lights" reported in 2023
  • First release of files: May 8, 2026
  • Expert opinion: Dr. Grant Denn, physics professor at MSU Denver

Timeline

The Pentagon has released multiple batches of UFO files under the PURSUE initiative. The first batch was made public on May 8, 2026. The latest release on August 7, 2026, includes multiple reports from various locations, including Colorado Springs.

The two sightings from Colorado Springs are categorized as follows: the first, "Dark Translucent Triangle," is based on a firsthand account from a former Department of Defense employee, while the second, "Large Triangle with Red Lights," was reported by a U.S. person at an unspecified time in 2023.

Official statements

“Not too exciting to me. They're mostly second-hand accounts with no photographic evidence or other corroboration between multiple parties. If something big flew over Colorado Springs the city would expect there would be hundreds of sightings of it instead of one or two now and then,” said Dr. Grant Denn, a physics professor at MSU Denver.
“Out there? Probably. The universe is... here. I don't think we're being visited. Space is so huge. Who would know that we're here?” Denn added when asked about the existence of life beyond Earth.

Context

The recent release of UFO files is part of a broader trend of increasing transparency from the U.S. government regarding UAPs. Previous incidents and reports have sparked public interest and debate about the existence of unidentified aerial phenomena.

Dr. Denn’s skepticism reflects a common viewpoint among experts, who often highlight the lack of corroborating evidence in many reported sightings.
