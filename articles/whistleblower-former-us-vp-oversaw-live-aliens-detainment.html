Whistleblower: Former US VP oversaw live aliens detainment

Military intelligence whistleblower David Grusch has claimed that late former US Vice President Dick Cheney oversaw a classified program involving the detainment and interaction with live extraterrestrials. Grusch made the allegations during an interview on The Dr. Phil Podcast, hosted by Phil McGraw, which aired recently.

This claim has raised significant concerns regarding government transparency and the public's right to access classified information. Authorities have not yet responded to these assertions, and the implications could affect ongoing discussions about unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAPs).

Key facts

  • Whistleblower: David Grusch, former US Air Force intelligence officer
  • Interview date: Not disclosed
  • Program overseen by: Dick Cheney, former US Vice President (2001-2009)
  • Estimated non-human craft in custody: Between 25 and 100
  • Cheney's death: November 3, 2025, due to pneumonia and other health issues
  • Statements from officials: Not disclosed

Timeline

David Grusch, a former intelligence officer, has testified before Congress regarding alleged secret UFO crash-retrieval programs. In a recent podcast interview, he detailed claims about Cheney's involvement in a program that allegedly included human interactions with non-human entities.

Grusch stated that he has spoken to high-ranking officials about these matters, although specific names and dates have not been disclosed. He described the communication with these entities as non-verbal, suggesting a form of consciousness communication akin to telepathy.

Official statements

“I know people who had direct interactions in a detainment situation, which sounds very Star Trek and sounds very crazy, but it is true,” said Grusch during the interview.
“David’s story is absolutely a reminder that asking hard questions and getting to the truth is every citizen’s right,” Phil McGraw emphasized.

Context

Grusch's claims come amid a broader discussion on UAPs and government transparency. The public has increasingly demanded access to classified information regarding unidentified aerial phenomena, especially following recent congressional hearings on the subject. No previous administration has openly acknowledged the existence of live extraterrestrial interactions.

What’s next

Investigations into Grusch's claims are ongoing, although specific timelines for any potential inquiries have not been disclosed. The U.S. Department of Defense has not yet validated his assertions.
