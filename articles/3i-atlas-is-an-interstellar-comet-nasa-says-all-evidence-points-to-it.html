3I/ATLAS Is An Interstellar Comet - NASA Says 'All Evidence Points To It'

Nasa has confirmed that Comet 3I/ATLAS is an interstellar comet, with all evidence supporting this claim, according to a statement made on October 30, 2023. The announcement was made by NASA associate administrator Amit Kshatriya and Dr. Nicky Fox, associate administrator of NASA Science, during a briefing that included new images from the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter.

This finding is significant as it enhances our understanding of the origins of celestial bodies in our solar system and beyond. NASA officials have stated that studying 3I/ATLAS can provide insights into the early solar system and the formation of other planetary systems.

Key facts

  • Date of announcement: October 30, 2023
  • Confirmed by: NASA
  • Key speakers: Amit Kshatriya, Dr. Nicky Fox
  • Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter involved in imaging

Timeline

On October 30, 2023, NASA officials held a briefing to announce that Comet 3I/ATLAS is confirmed as an interstellar comet. The announcement included new imagery captured by the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter.

Prior to this announcement, 3I/ATLAS had been observed by various telescopes, leading to speculation about its origins. NASA's confirmation provides clarity on its status as an interstellar object.

Official statements

“All evidence points to 3I/ATLAS being an interstellar comet,” said Amit Kshatriya during the briefing.
“This discovery opens new avenues for understanding the formation of our solar system,” Dr. Nicky Fox added.

Context

Comet 3I/ATLAS is part of a growing list of interstellar objects observed in recent years. Its discovery follows the notable interstellar object 'Oumuamua, which was identified in 2017. Both discoveries challenge existing theories about the formation and movement of celestial bodies.

What’s next

Nasa plans to continue studying 3I/ATLAS as it approaches the inner solar system. Further observations will aim to gather more data on its composition and trajectory.
