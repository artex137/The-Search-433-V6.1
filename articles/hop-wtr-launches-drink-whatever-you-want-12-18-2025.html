Hop Wtr Launches 'Drink Whatever You Want' Campaign Ahead of Comet 3I ATLAS

Hop Wtr has launched a new campaign titled "Drink Whatever You Want" in anticipation of the interstellar comet 3I ATLAS passing by Earth on December 19, 2025. The campaign features a partnership with Dr. Steven Greer, a former physician and founder of the Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence, who discusses the comet and the potential for extraterrestrial life.

This campaign is significant as it coincides with Dry January, a time when many people abstain from alcohol. Hop Wtr aims to position itself as a fun alternative to alcoholic beverages during the holiday season and January, encouraging consumers to enjoy themselves without restrictions.

Key facts

  • Campaign launch date: December 2025
  • Comet 3I ATLAS will pass by Earth on December 19, 2025
  • Partnership with Dr. Steven Greer, founder of the Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence
  • Merchandise includes reflective hats, “alien-proof” jackets, a “The Aliens Have Landed” hoodie, and a “Dr. Greer” T-shirt
  • Hop Wtr co-founder and CEO: Jordan Bass

Timeline

On December 19, 2025, the comet 3I ATLAS will make its closest approach to Earth. In anticipation of this event, Hop Wtr has rolled out its "Drink Whatever You Want" campaign, which includes various promotional activities featuring Dr. Greer. The campaign encourages consumers to enjoy beverages without the fear of restriction during the festive season.

Official statements

“The holiday season and Dry January should feel celebratory, not stressful or restrictive," said Jordan Bass, co-founder and CEO of Hop Wtr. "This year, everyone is talking about mysterious objects in the sky and the possibility of an alien touchdown, so we thought why not have fun with it?”

Context

Dry January is a popular movement encouraging people to abstain from alcohol for the month. Many brands use this opportunity to promote non-alcoholic alternatives. Hop Wtr's campaign stands out by incorporating themes of extraterrestrial life and humor, appealing to consumers' curiosity during a time of festive celebrations.

What’s next

As the comet approaches, Hop Wtr will continue its promotional activities, leveraging the campaign to attract consumers looking for refreshing beverage options during the holiday season and into January.
